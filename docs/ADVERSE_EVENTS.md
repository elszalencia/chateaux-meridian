# Adverse events

What happens when a plan does not go as scheduled: a late or missed installment, a withdrawal, a frozen balance, a payment that comes back. For each case this page gives the trigger, the state the `payment_plan` contract ends in, what happens off chain, where the code hooks in, and the business decision that is still open.

**Status of the policy.** The mechanics are built and tested; the business rules are not decided. The running policy is `NullPolicy` (`services/src/policy/NullPolicy.ts`): it applies only the grace and missed rules the brief fixes (5 business days of grace, brief §9) and records everything else for a person. It never refunds, never charges a penalty, never declares a default and never cancels. Every event it writes carries `policyStatus: 'pending'`.

## The pieces

| Piece | Where | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plan states | `contracts/payment_plan/src/lib.rs` `PlanState` | `Active`, `Delinquent`, `Defaulted`, `OnHold`, `Cancelled`, `Completed`. Funds move only in `Active` and `Delinquent`. |
| Installment states | `lib.rs` `Status` | `Open`, `Locked`, `Released`, `Broken`, plus `Missed` and `Waived`. |
| Per-plan policy | `lib.rs` `PolicyConfig` | `grace_days`, `misses_before_default`, `penalty_bps`, `refund_mode`. The last two are recorded, not applied on chain. |
| Gated functions | `lib.rs` `mark_missed`, `cure`, `waive`, `declare_default`, `place_hold`, `lift_hold`, `hold_frozen`, `cancel_plan`, `refund`, `complete_plan` | Each takes a reason code and a 32-byte evidence hash and emits an event. `cancel_plan` is admin only, `hold_frozen` is permissionless (it verifies the freeze itself), the rest are operator only. `refund` can only pay the plan's buyer. |
| Reason codes | `services/src/policy/reasons.ts` `REASONS` | 1xx installment and default, 2xx cancellation, 3xx holds, 4xx refunds, 5xx closing. The web app turns each into plain language (`reasons.*` in `web/src/i18n`). |
| Rule mirror | `services/src/policy/transitions.ts` `applyTransition` | The contract's transition rules in TypeScript, used for the mock provider and to dry-run every action before it is sent. |
| Contract seam | `services/src/policy/GovernanceProvider.ts` `GovernanceProvider` | One method per contract function. `MockGovernanceProvider` in mock mode; the testnet implementation maps onto the chain client. |
| Single execution path | `services/src/policy/adverseEvents.ts` `performAdverseAction` | Dry-run, evidence hash, provider call, then the store write and one `policy_events` row. |
| Daily job | `adverseEvents.ts` `runAdverseEventsDaily` | Asks the policy engine about every plan whose funds can move and executes the answers; registered by the job registry as `adverse-events-daily`. |
| Policy seam | `services/src/policy/PolicyEngine.ts` `PolicyEngine` | `evaluate(facts)` returns decisions; `sizeRefund(facts, reason)` sizes a refund or returns `null` for a person to size it. |
| Views | `services/src/api/views.ts` `governanceView` | Buyer notices and the builder's state, reason and event log. |

## Cases

| # | Case | Trigger | On-chain state | Off-chain action | Hook (file:function) | Open policy decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Late, inside grace | Due date passed, installment unpaid, still within 5 business days | None: the installment stays `Open` or `Locked`; plan stays `Active` | `NullPolicy` rule R1 records a `late_in_grace` event once; the buyer sees "La cuota {n} está pendiente" with the date grace ends and that paying now is enough; nothing is charged | `services/src/policy/NullPolicy.ts:NullPolicy.evaluate` (R1); notice in `services/src/api/views.ts:governanceView` | D1: Is a late fee charged inside grace, and is the buyer reminded (and how often) before grace ends? |
| 2 | Missed, past grace | Unpaid beyond 5 business days and beyond the plan's `grace_days` | `mark_missed` (reason 100): installment `Missed`, plan `Delinquent`, miss counted. A `Locked` installment is broken first by the break job, then marked | R2 requests `mark_missed` through the provider; the buyer sees "La cuota {n} quedó sin pagar", that the plan is still in force, and to write to their advisor to catch up; payments of that installment are refused until it is cured | `NullPolicy.ts:NullPolicy.evaluate` (R2) → `services/src/policy/adverseEvents.ts:runAdverseEventsDaily` → `performAdverseAction` | D2: Late interest or penalty on a missed installment; whether the contract's calendar `grace_days` should become business days to match the brief. |
| 3 | Repeated misses → default | Outstanding misses reach `misses_before_default` (3 by default) | `declare_default` (reason 110), only when a person approves it: plan `Defaulted`; no funds move except `refund` to the buyer | R3 records `default_eligible` only; the builder sees the flag; a person runs `declare_default` | `NullPolicy.ts:NullPolicy.evaluate` (R3); action via `adverseEvents.ts:performAdverseAction` | D3: Threshold (3?), who approves a default, what notice period applies, and what happens to the held balance and to the encargo (restitution, penalty). |
| 4 | Cure | Buyer pays or agrees a catch-up on a missed installment | `cure` (reason 101): installment back to `Open` (payable at spot); plan back to `Active` when no misses remain. Not allowed from `Defaulted` | Operator action; buyer notice disappears | `adverseEvents.ts:performAdverseAction` with `{ kind: 'cure' }` | D4: Can a defaulted plan be reinstated (the contract refuses today), and is the cure at today's rate or at the lock's rate? |
| 5 | Waive | The builder forgives an installment (e.g. a delay credit) | `waive` (reason 102): installment `Waived`; counts as done for `complete_plan` | Operator action; the builder view counts waived installments | `adverseEvents.ts:performAdverseAction` with `{ kind: 'waive' }` | D5: Who may waive, up to what amount, and how a waiver is reflected in the encargo. |
| 6 | Buyer withdrawal (desistimiento) | Buyer signs a desistimiento | `cancel_plan` (reason 200, admin): plan `Cancelled`; then `refund` (reason 400) only to the buyer, in one or several parts | The refund is sized by a person: `NullPolicy.sizeRefund` returns `null` and states that the encargo's penalty clause (`penalty_bps`, 10 % recorded) is not applied automatically | `adverseEvents.ts:performAdverseAction` (`cancel_plan`, `refund`); sizing in `NullPolicy.ts:NullPolicy.sizeRefund` | D6: Penalty from the encargo clause (percentage of deposits or of the price), whether it is withheld from the USDC balance or settled by the trust in pesos, and the FX rate used for the refund. |
| 7 | Project cancelled, refunds | The builder cancels the project | `cancel_plan` (reason 201) for every plan of the project, then `refund` of the full held balance to each buyer | Batch of operator actions; buyers see "Tu plan fue cancelado" and the amount held in their name | `adverseEvents.ts:performAdverseAction` per plan | D7: Refund in USD or in pesos through the trust, who bears the FX difference and the hedge unwind cost, and any compensation owed. |
| 8 | Mortgage-phase foreclosure or restitution | Default on the bank mortgage after the down payment | None. The contract covers the down-payment installments only (brief §8, no phase field) | Record only: the builder writes a note; no contract call | No hook (out of scope; record in the trust's systems) | D8: Whether Casa Puente needs any record of the mortgage phase at all. |
| 9 | ACH return after USDC was credited | The US bank returns the ACH debit (R01, R10…) after the on-ramp credited USDC and it was deposited | `place_hold` (reason 303): plan `OnHold`; deposits, locks and releases refused. If already released to the payee, nothing on chain can undo it | The on-ramp status poller sees the return and requests the hold; the buyer sees "Tu plan está en pausa" (reason: "El banco devolvió un pago") and that their advisor will contact them | Provider call via `adverseEvents.ts:performAdverseAction`; the trigger belongs in the on-ramp status handling of the anchor integration | D9: Who carries the loss when USDC was already released, whether the installment is reopened or marked missed, and whether the buyer may keep paying by ACH. |
| 10 | SEP-31 payout expired or refunded | The Colombian anchor cannot pay the trust (expired, refunded) | The installment is already `Released` to the payee backend; the returned USDC arrives back at the payee, not at the plan | The payout status handler records reason 305 and retries the payout or places a hold; reconciliation shows "Diferencia" until resolved | Reason `PAYOUT_RETURNED` in `reasons.ts`; trigger in the SEP-31 status handling of the anchor integration | D10: Retry window and count, whether the buyer is told, and who pays a second payout fee. |
| 11 | KYC or sanctions status change | The bank, the anchor or a screening list flags the buyer | `place_hold` (reason 300 KYC, 301 sanctions): plan `OnHold`; `lift_hold` returns it to its previous state | Operator action after the review; buyer sees "Tu plan está en pausa" (reason: "Verificación de identidad en curso") | `adverseEvents.ts:performAdverseAction` (`place_hold`, `lift_hold`) | D11: Who decides, the maximum hold duration, and what happens to a sanctions match (reporting, refund prohibited, funds held). |
| 12 | Frozen USDC | The USDC issuer deauthorizes the vault, the payee or the buyer (Circle's USDC is `auth_revocable`, not clawback-enabled) | Any transfer fails with `AssetFrozen` and writes nothing; `hold_frozen` (reason 304, permissionless) then moves an `Active` or `Delinquent` plan `OnHold`; `lift_hold` refuses while the freeze lasts | The daily job calls `tryHoldFrozen` for each plan before evaluating it, and after any `AssetFrozen` error | `adverseEvents.ts:runAdverseEventsDaily` and `tryHoldFrozen`; contract `lib.rs:hold_frozen` | D12: How the buyer is told, whether installments falling due during the freeze are marked missed or suspended, and the legal route with the issuer. |
| 13 | Disputed payment | The buyer, the bank or the trust disputes an installment | `place_hold` (reason 302) | Operator action; reconciliation row stays "Pendiente" | `adverseEvents.ts:performAdverseAction` (`place_hold`) | D13: Dispute process, time limits and who arbitrates. |
| — | Completed | Every installment `Released` or `Waived` | `complete_plan` (reason 500): plan `Completed`; leftover balance returned to the buyer | Operator action | `adverseEvents.ts:performAdverseAction` (`complete_plan`) | — |

## Guarantees and their tests

- **No stuck plan on a freeze.** A failed transfer due to deauthorization is a typed error, never a silent failure, and the plan can be moved `OnHold` by anyone. Tests: `test::frozen::*` in `contracts/payment_plan/src/test_frozen.rs`; `runAdverseEventsDaily › a frozen plan is put on hold (reason 304) instead of being worked on`.
- **Funds are conserved.** Adverse transitions move money only between "held" and "refunded to the buyer". Tests: `test::adverse::funds_are_conserved_across_two_plans_through_every_adverse_case`; `runAdverseEventsDaily › applies the grace and missed rules through the provider and conserves funds`; `operator actions and funds conservation › *`.
- **Refunds can only reach the buyer.** Test: `test::adverse::cancel_plan_is_admin_only_and_the_refund_can_only_reach_the_buyer`.
- **The daily job is idempotent.** Test: `runAdverseEventsDaily › is idempotent: a second run the same day changes nothing`.
- **Every action is evidenced.** The evidence hash is SHA-256 over the canonical record (`services/src/policy/evidence.ts:evidenceHash`), stored in `policy_events` and sent to the contract, which emits it.

## Open policy decisions

D1–D13 above. Until they are answered, `NullPolicy` stays in place and every non-mechanical step is a person's action.
